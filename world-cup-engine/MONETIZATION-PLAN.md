# 90-Day Plan to Monetization — The Lost XI

## Starting point (strong)
- **~3,000 subscribers, clean channel (no strikes).**
- That means the **subscriber threshold is already cleared** (500 for entry, 1,000 for
  ad revenue). The only thing left is **views/watch-time** + housekeeping.

## The target
| Tier | Needs | Unlocks |
|------|-------|---------|
| **Entry** (first goal) | 500 subs ✅ + 3 uploads/90d + **3,000 watch hrs OR 3M Shorts views** (90d) | Super Thanks, memberships |
| **Full ad revenue** | 1,000 subs ✅ + **4,000 watch hrs (12mo) OR 10M Shorts views (90d)** | Ad money |

**Primary route: Shorts views.** 3M in 90 days ≈ **~33,000 views/day** average. With a
3k-sub base giving every Short an initial push + the World Cup tailwind, that's reachable
if we post daily and a few Shorts pop. Long-form runs in parallel to bank watch-hours
(the alternative path + where real CPM lives later).

## Cadence
- **3–5 Shorts/day** (from the 30-Short backlog + daily World Cup newsjacks).
- **1 long-form/week** (the 6-episode backlog), each clipped into more Shorts.
- Every Short: punchy hook in the first 1.5s, voiceover, captions, royalty-free/trending audio.

## Rebrand steps (do from your phone, ~5 min)
1. Rename channel → **The Lost XI**, set handle (`@thelostxi`).
2. Upload **PFP** + **banner**, paste **About** text — all in `CHANNEL-KIT.md`.
3. Heads-up: a niche pivot may dip your reach for ~1–2 weeks as football fans replace
   your old audience. Normal. Don't panic — push through it.

## Housekeeping (required for YPP)
- [ ] Turn on **2-step verification** (Google account).
- [ ] Keep channel **strike-free** (clean ✅).
- [ ] Link an **AdSense** account when you apply.
- [ ] Apply to YPP the moment views/hours hit the bar (YouTube Studio → Earn).

## What pushes the numbers (from the research)
- Post daily/near-daily — biggest single growth lever.
- Front-load every hook (our 15s test scored 27/100 on hook because it opened slow —
  the engine fixes this with a first-1.5s hook → aim 70+).
- Ride **verified** viral World Cup moments within the hour.
- Double down weekly on whichever hooks/topics over-perform.

## Honest expectation
Entry-tier eligibility in ~**4–10 weeks** is realistic if we stay consistent and land a
few hits. Full ad revenue + meaningful money is a months-long build. The win this summer
is **crossing the monetization line and banking a football audience** during the Cup.
