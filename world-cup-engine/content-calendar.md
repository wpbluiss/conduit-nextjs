# Content Calendar — Launch Backlog

Cadence (from the research): **1 long-form/week + ~5 Shorts/day**, each long-form
clipped into 5 Shorts. This backlog = **6 long-form episodes + 30 Shorts** ≈ the first
6 weeks of long-form and ~6 days of Shorts to start (then keep clipping).

## Long-form schedule (post 1/week, ride the World Cup)
| Week | Episode | Bucket | World Cup peg |
|------|---------|--------|---------------|
| Launch / kickoff | **ep01 — The First World Cup (1930)** | History | Tournament starts |
| 1 | **ep03 — The Maracanazo (1950)** | Disaster | "biggest collapse ever" |
| 2 | **ep05 — Andrés Escobar (1994)** | Tragedy | USA hosted '94 → 2026 USA peg |
| 3 | **ep02 — Garrincha** | Forgotten genius | evergreen |
| 4 | **ep04 — The Football War (1969)** | Scandal/rivalry | qualifier drama |
| 5 | **ep06 — Senegal 2002** | Underdog | feel-good balance |

> Mix dark (tragedy/disaster) with uplifting (underdog/genius) so the channel isn't
> relentlessly grim — variety also helps the "inauthentic content" safety (genuinely
> varied episodes).

## Shorts schedule (5/day)
Each long-form ships with 5 Shorts (in its episode file). Post them across the days
following that week's long-form, plus **newsjack Shorts** whenever a *verified* viral
World Cup moment happens (use the NEWSJACK-FILTER prompt). Always:
- Hook + on-screen text in the first frame (<3s).
- Voiceover + royalty-free music (never licensed songs).
- Looped payoff + 👇 CTA.
- End screen / pinned comment pointing to the full long-form (drives watch-hours).

## Daily loop (once the engine is deployed)
1. Engine picks next queued script → renders → uploads as **private**.
2. You get a "ready to publish" ping → glance → tap publish (~30s each).
3. Engine logs performance; weekly, double down on whichever hooks/topics over-performed.
