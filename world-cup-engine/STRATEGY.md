# 30-Day World Cup Growth Strategy

Goal: grow a faceless World Cup channel as fast as possible during the 2026
tournament, **without** doing the things that get channels deleted.

---

## The monetization target (what "winning" means in 30 days)

As of 2026, YouTube has a tiered Partner Program:

| Tier | Requirement | What it unlocks |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Entry** | 500 subs **+** 3,000 watch hours **OR 3M Shorts views (90 days)** + 3 uploads | Fan funding (Super Thanks, memberships) |
| **Full** | 1,000 subs **+** 4,000 watch hours **OR 10M Shorts views (90 days)** | Full ad revenue share |

**Our path: the Shorts-views lane.** A daily-posting channel in a white-hot
niche can realistically chase **3M Shorts views in 90 days**. That's the 30-day
objective — *qualify*, bank subs + views — not a big payout this month.

Sources: [TubeBuddy](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-monetization-requirements/),
[vidIQ Shorts](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization/).

## Why no footage <a name="why-no-footage"></a>

This is the section to re-read whenever the temptation to "just post the
highlight" comes back.

1. **Match highlights → copyright strikes → deletion.** FIFA and the
   broadcasters fingerprint every frame with Content ID. Even under YouTube's
   official FIFA 2026 partnership, **full matches stay with broadcasters** —
   only *official broadcasters* may post the first 10 minutes + select matches.
   A normal channel posting clips gets struck. 3 strikes = terminated.
   ([Dawan](https://www.dawan.africa/news/fifa-taps-youtube-for-world-cup-2026-but-full-matches-stay-with-broadcasters))
2. **Copyrighted songs → $0 revenue.** The Shakira/IShowSpeed tracks are
   Content-ID'd. The system diverts the money to the label. You can get a
   million views and earn nothing.
3. **Reposting viral clips → "inauthentic content" termination.** YouTube
   renamed its policy to "inauthentic content" in July 2025 and in **January
   2026** ran its largest-ever enforcement wave, terminating channels with a
   combined **4.7B views / 35M subs / ~$10M/yr**, for exactly this: scraping
   viral clips, auto-spinning, mass-posting.
   ([CineD](https://www.cined.com/youtube-set-to-crack-down-on-ai-slop-with-monetization-policy-update/))

**Key nuance:** AI/automation is *not* banned. *Templated, repetitive,
no-original-input* content is. So we automate aggressively but make every video
carry original commentary, data, or visuals — that's the line between "scaled"
and "slop."

## Content pillars (all original, all fast)

The agent runs these formats on the *same* viral moments your repost idea would
have chased — just with original material:

1. **Breaking-take Shorts** — a real, *verified* World Cup news event (transfer,
   injury, lineup, drama, a genuine viral moment) → 20–35s original spoken take
   over AI-generated/licensed visuals + bold on-screen text. *This is the
   newsjacking engine.* (Verified events only — fabricating "Messi quits" is a
   misinformation strike.)
2. **Stat bombs** — "X has scored in every WC knockout since 2018" over original
   motion-graphics. Data is facts (not copyrightable); the presentation is ours.
3. **Predictions / scenarios** — group-stage permutations, "who advances if…",
   bracket simulations.
4. **Player-to-watch profiles** — original narrated profile + generated visuals.
5. **Tactical explainers** — formations, set-pieces, drawn as original diagrams.

Long-form (1–2x/week) for watch-hours: 8–12 min "everything you need before
[match]" / "ranking all 48 teams" using the same original-asset rule.

## Cadence

- **Shorts: 3–5/day**, front-loaded around match kickoffs and any viral spike.
- **Newsjack: within ~60 min** of a verified viral moment (the radar triggers it).
- **Long-form: 1–2/week.**
- Every post passes the **2-minute approval gate** before publishing (see
  [`PIPELINE.md`](./PIPELINE.md)) — this is what keeps us off the Jan-2026 ban
  pattern while staying near-autonomous.

---

## Packaging (where Shorts are actually won)

- **Hook in the first 1.5s** — text + spoken, state the stakes immediately.
- **Retention** — hard cuts every 2–3s, on-screen text always moving, no dead air.
- **Loop** — last line feeds back into the first for replays.
- **Title/thumbnail** — one bold claim, one face/crest/number, high contrast.
- Run every concept through the **virality predictor** tool before rendering;
  only produce the ones that score well on hook + retention.
