# Phone Setup — revid.ai (or AutoShorts) → real fast-cut narrated Shorts

This is the tool that makes the video you actually want: AI voiceover + quick scene
cuts + bold captions + music, built from a script. Works entirely in your phone browser.
~15 minutes to set up, then each video is ~2 minutes.

## A) revid.ai (recommended — best control)
1. Phone browser → **revid.ai** → sign up (Google login is fine).
2. Tap **Create Video → "Faceless Video"** (or "Script to Video").
3. **Paste a VOICEOVER script** from `shorts-scripts.md` (just the VOICEOVER line).
4. Set these options:
   - **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (vertical)
   - **Voice:** a deep, serious documentary male voice (preview a few — pick the most cinematic)
   - **Captions:** ON — bold, word-by-word/animated, centered
   - **Visuals/B-roll:** AI images or stock, "cinematic / vintage / historical" style
   - **Music:** cinematic, dramatic, **volume low** (so the voice leads)
   - **Speed/pacing:** fast / dynamic if offered
5. **Generate.** Review the preview. Regenerate any weak scene.
6. **Download** the MP4 (or use revid's auto-post/schedule to YouTube on paid tiers).
7. Repeat with the next script. Do 3–5/day.

Pricing: paid plans roughly **$20–40/mo** (credit-based). Start on the cheapest that
covers ~30–60 videos/mo.

## B) AutoShorts.ai (alternative — most hands-off)
- autoshorts.ai → sign up → it can **auto-generate + auto-post daily** to YouTube on a
  schedule. Less manual, less control. ~$19–69/mo. Use our scripts/topics as the input.

## Settings that make it go viral (from the research)
- **Hook in the first line** — every script here already opens on the hook. Keep it first.
- **Captions ON, big and bold** — most Shorts are watched on mute.
- **A cut every 2–3 seconds** — pick the "dynamic/fast" visual pacing.
- **Voice leads, music sits under it** — keep music volume low.
- **Add a trending sound option:** if a video underperforms, re-upload it in the YouTube
  app and swap in a trending track (mute original, Add sound).

## Which to post first
Start with the three strongest hooks: **#1 One-Armed Hero, #2 The Game That Started a
War, #3 The Own Goal That Cost His Life.** They have the hardest hooks and broadest pull.

## Reminder
Fact-check each script's claims before posting — a history channel lives or dies on
accuracy. All facts in `shorts-scripts.md` are verifiable; just confirm specifics.
