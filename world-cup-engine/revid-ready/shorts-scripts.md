captioned** Shorts from a script — i.e. the video you actually want (voiceover + quick
scene changes + bold captions + music), NOT a slow single-image pan.

**How to use each one:** in revid.ai (or AutoShorts) choose "Script → Video" / "Faceless
video", paste the **VOICEOVER** text, then set: vertical 9:16, captions ON (bold,
word-by-word), a deep documentary male voice, cinematic background music at low volume,
AI/stock visuals. The app cuts the scenes, voices it, and captions it automatically.

Each script is built on the viral formula: **hook in the first line (<3s) → fast
escalation → payoff → loop.** Keep narration tight; the app cuts a new visual every line.
