## Setup steps (phone, ~5 min)
1. YouTube app → your channel → **Edit** (or youtube.com on a browser → Customize channel).
2. **Picture** → upload the PFP. **Banner** → upload the banner.
3. **About/Description** → paste the text above. Add your business email.
4. Set the **handle** (Settings → Channel → handle).
5. Upload the **first Short** (mp4 link above): YouTube app → Create → upload the video →
   title `Football's One-Armed Hero 💪 #WorldCup2026` → in the editor, tap **Add sound**
   and pick any track from YouTube's library (free + safe) → post.

> Heads-up: the first Short is a short visual teaser (motion + on-screen text + a
> trending sound you add in-app — no editing needed). The full narrated version comes
> once the auto-engine is deployed (see DEPLOY.md).
