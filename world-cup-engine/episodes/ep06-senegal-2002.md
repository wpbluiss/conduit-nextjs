# Episode 06 — "The Greatest Upset in World Cup History" (Senegal, 2002)

**Bucket:** Underdogs (the uplifting one — balance the dark stories). **Length:** ~6–8
min. **Why it works:** pure feel-good shock; debutants humble the world champions.

## Long-form narration script
> **HOOK:** They had never played a World Cup match in their lives. Their opponents were
> the reigning world champions, packed with the best players on the planet. The whole
> world expected a massacre. Ninety minutes later, the champions were beaten — and an
> entire continent was dancing.
>
> 2002. The World Cup opens in South Korea and Japan, and the very first match is a
> coronation: France, the defending champions, the team that had won the World Cup *and*
> the European Championship, against tiny Senegal — a nation playing in its first ever
> World Cup. Most of the Senegal squad had grown up idolizing the French stars they were
> about to face. Many of them even played their club football in France.
>
> The pundits didn't ask *if* France would win. They asked by how many.
>
> **(THE TURN)** And then Senegal did something nobody had scripted. They ran. They
> fought. They believed. Just past the half-hour mark, a scramble in the French box, and
> Papa Bouba Diop bundled the ball over the line. Senegal 1, France 0. He ran to the
> corner, took off his shirt, laid it on the grass, and the whole team danced around it.
>
> France threw everything forward. It didn't matter. Senegal held on. The world
> champions had lost their opening game to a team of debutants. And Senegal weren't done
> — they went all the way to the quarter-finals, one of the great World Cup runs ever.
>
> It's still the blueprint every underdog dreams of: that on one night, with enough
> heart, anyone can beat anyone.
>
> **CLIFFHANGER:** Next: the team that did the impossible at home — and made an entire
> nation believe.

## 5 Shorts
1. **"Their FIRST ever World Cup game — vs the world champions"** — the setup.
2. **"They grew up idolizing the team they just beat"** — the emotional twist.
3. **"The greatest shirt-on-the-grass celebration ever 💃"** — Bouba Diop's dance.
4. **"Debutants → the QUARTER-FINALS"** — how far they went.
5. **"The blueprint every underdog dreams of"** — the inspirational closer.
