# Episode 02 — "The Genius With Broken Legs" (Garrincha)

**Bucket:** Forgotten Players / rise-and-fall. **Length:** ~6–8 min long-form.
**Why it works:** "the greatest dribbler ever, born deformed, died broke" is a pure
curiosity-gap rise-and-fall. World Cup tie-in (1958 & 1962). Copyright-safe — scarce
footage means AI/illustration visuals are expected.

## Long-form narration script
> **HOOK:** Doctors said he would never walk properly. His spine was twisted, one leg
> bent outward, the other shorter. They were right about the walking. They had no
> idea he was about to become the greatest dribbler football has ever seen.
>
> They called him Garrincha — "the little bird." A boy from a poor town in Brazil,
> with legs so crooked that defenders simply could not read which way he'd go… because
> *he* didn't know either. He played on instinct, on joy. The crowd didn't just want
> Brazil to win. They wanted to see Garrincha *humiliate* people. And he did.
>
> In 1958, a 24-year-old Garrincha helped Brazil win their first World Cup. But 1962
> is where the legend was carved. Brazil's biggest star, Pelé, got injured early in
> the tournament. The team should have collapsed. Instead, Garrincha picked them up
> and carried them on his crooked legs — scoring, dribbling past entire defenses,
> dragging Brazil to a second world title almost by himself.
>
> They called him *Alegria do Povo* — the joy of the people. He didn't play for money
> or fame. People said he played like a child in a backyard who never grew up.
>
> And that was the problem. **(THE TURN)** Off the pitch, the same man who could
> dance past four defenders couldn't escape the bottle. As the cheering faded, the
> drinking grew. The money vanished. The marriages broke. The body that defied doctors
> as a boy gave out early.
>
> In 1983, Garrincha died — 49 years old, broke, and largely alone. The joy of the
> people had run out of joy. Tens of thousands lined the streets for his funeral. On
> his grave, they wrote: *"Here rests the man who was the joy of the people."*
>
> **CLIFFHANGER:** He won two World Cups with a smile. Our next story is about a man
> who lost one — and paid for it with his life.

## 5 Shorts
1. **"Doctors said he'd never walk. He won 2 World Cups."** — the deformity → genius hook.
2. **"When Pelé got injured in 1962, ONE man carried Brazil"** — Garrincha's solo tournament.
3. **"Defenders couldn't read him because HE didn't know where he was going"** — the dribble myth.
4. **"They called him 'the joy of the people' 🐦"** — the nickname + crowd love.
5. **"The saddest grave in football"** — died broke at 49, the epitaph.
