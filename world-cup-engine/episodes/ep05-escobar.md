# Episode 05 — "The Own Goal That Cost a Life" (Andrés Escobar, 1994)

**Bucket:** Tragedy. **Length:** ~6–8 min. **Why it works:** the most haunting story
in World Cup history; strong 2026 peg (USA hosts again; 1994 is the last US-hosted Cup,
+32 years). Handle with respect — a real man was killed.

## Long-form narration script
> **HOOK:** He was 27, handsome, calm, beloved — they called him "The Gentleman of
> Football." Ten days after he touched the ball in a World Cup match, he was shot dead
> in a parking lot. The killer is said to have shouted one word with each shot: "Goal."
>
> 1994. Colombia arrive at the World Cup in the United States as one of the favorites —
> a golden generation, tipped by some to win the whole thing. At the heart of their
> defense is Andrés Escobar: elegant, professional, a leader. The kind of player every
> country wishes it had.
>
> Then, in a group-stage match against the host nation, the unthinkable. Stretching to
> cut out a cross, Escobar turns the ball into his *own* net. An own goal. The USA win.
> Colombia — the favorites — are knocked out in the first round. Humiliated. Sent home.
>
> **(THE TURN)** Back in Colombia, in a country awash with cartel money and illegal
> betting, that elimination had cost powerful people fortunes. Days later, outside a bar
> in Medellín, Escobar was confronted, and shot multiple times. Witnesses said the
> gunman taunted him with the word "Gol" — goal — as he fired.
>
> Andrés Escobar was murdered for a mistake in a game. More than 100,000 people came to
> mourn him. His death became a symbol — of a beautiful sport, and of a country at the
> mercy of forces far bigger than football.
>
> He once wrote, after the own goal, in a final newspaper column: *"Life doesn't end
> here. We have to go on. See you soon, because life does not end here."* Days later, it did.
>
> **CLIFFHANGER:** That's the dark side of the dream. Next: the most beautiful upset the
> World Cup has ever seen.

## 5 Shorts
1. **"He scored an own goal. 10 days later he was murdered."** — the brutal premise.
2. **"They called him 'The Gentleman of Football' 🕊️"** — who he was.
3. **"Colombia were FAVORITES in 1994 — out in the first round"** — the fall.
4. **"His last words, written days before he died"** — the column quote.
5. **"100,000 people came to say goodbye"** — the mourning.

## Fact-check + sensitivity note
Andrés Escobar (Colombia): own goal vs USA, 1994 WC group stage; Colombia eliminated;
murdered in Medellín on July 2, 1994. Treat with dignity — verify every detail
(especially the "Gol" account and the column quote) before narration; do not
sensationalize the violence.
