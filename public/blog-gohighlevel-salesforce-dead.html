Why GoHighLevel, Salesforce, and HubSpot Are Already Obsolete

By Luis Garcia, Founder of Conduit AI — April 3, 2026

GoHighLevel. Salesforce. HubSpot. Zoho. Monday.com. These platforms promised to simplify business operations by putting everything in one place. And for a while, they did. But there is a fundamental flaw in their model: they give you tools and expect you to do the work.

A CRM does not sell for you. An email marketing platform does not write your campaigns. A project management tool does not manage your projects. These platforms organize work. They do not perform it. You still need to hire people to use them. And you end up paying for the software AND the people.

The Tool vs. The Worker

Here is the fundamental difference. Salesforce gives you a CRM that your sales team uses to track deals. Conduit gives you AI sales employees that find leads, qualify them, manage the pipeline, follow up automatically, and update the records themselves. One is a tool. The other is a worker.

HubSpot gives you marketing automation tools that your marketing team configures, monitors, and optimizes. Conduit gives you AI marketing employees that create the content, set up the campaigns, run the A/B tests, and optimize based on performance data. Without anyone configuring anything.

GoHighLevel gives you a platform that your team needs to learn, set up, and operate. Conduit gives you a workforce that operates itself.

The Hidden Cost of Platform Complexity

Every business that adopts GoHighLevel, Salesforce, or HubSpot discovers the same thing: the platform itself requires significant time and expertise to use effectively. You need to build workflows. You need to customize pipelines. You need to create templates. You need to set up integrations. You need to train your team on how to use it.

Many businesses end up hiring GoHighLevel specialists or HubSpot consultants just to get value from the platform they are already paying for. The total cost of ownership is the subscription plus the salary of the person running it plus the opportunity cost of the time spent configuring it instead of doing actual business work.

AI Employees Eliminate the Middle Layer

When AI employees handle your sales, marketing, finance, HR, legal, operations, and customer success, you do not need a CRM because the AI manages relationships directly. You do not need marketing automation because the AI is the marketer. You do not need project management software because the AI manages its own tasks.

The entire category of business software tools becomes unnecessary when the work itself is automated. You do not need a better hammer when you have a robot that builds the house.

The Migration Is Happening Now

Early adopters are already canceling their GoHighLevel subscriptions, letting their Salesforce contracts lapse, and downgrading their HubSpot plans. Not because those platforms are bad but because they solved yesterday's problem. Yesterday's problem was organizing work. Today's solution is eliminating the need for humans to do the work in the first place.

The all-in-one platform era is over. The AI workforce era has begun.
