Building in Public: How a 21-Year-Old With No CS Degree Built a Virtual Corporation

By Luis Garcia, Founder of Conduit AI — March 19, 2026

Six weeks. No venture capital. No engineering team. No computer science degree. That is the unconventional origin story of Conduit AI, a platform that deploys 33 autonomous AI employees across 9 departments to run your entire business.

My name is Luis Garcia. I am 21 years old and I built Conduit AI from scratch because I saw something the enterprise tech world was missing: small businesses do not need better tools. They need workers who actually do the work.

6
Weeks from first line of code to a fully operational AI workforce platform

The Problem I Could Not Ignore

I spent time working with small business owners: plumbers, restaurant owners, insurance agents, salon operators. Every single one had the same problem. They were drowning in work that had nothing to do with their actual expertise. The plumber was spending half his day answering phones and writing invoices instead of fixing pipes. The salon owner was staying up late managing social media instead of sleeping.

The tech industry's solution was more software tools. A CRM here. An email platform there. A project management tool. A scheduling app. But tools do not solve the labor problem. Tools still require humans to operate them. What these businesses needed was not another dashboard. They needed employees they could not afford to hire.

Building the Solution

I started building Conduit AI with a clear thesis: create AI employees that perform specific job functions autonomously. Not chatbots that answer questions. Not workflow automation that triggers sequences. Actual employees that make decisions, take actions, and produce outputs without human supervision.

The architecture came together in focused, obsessive sprints. Night after night, weekend after weekend, building and testing and rebuilding. No product meetings. No design sprints. No standups. Just relentless execution.

What People Told Me Was Impossible

When I described what I was building, the responses followed a pattern. People with traditional tech backgrounds told me it was too ambitious. People with business backgrounds told me small businesses would never trust AI to run their operations. People with VC experience told me I needed a team, funding, and a runway.

I ignored all of it. Not out of arrogance but because I could see that the technology was ready and the need was desperate. The gap between what AI could do and what businesses were using it for was enormous. Someone just needed to close it.

Why No CS Degree Does Not Matter

I did not study computer science in college. What I did was build. Every day. For years. The skills I needed came from solving real problems, not from sitting in lectures. The understanding of business operations came from talking to actual business owners, not from reading case studies.

The best products are not built by people with the most credentials. They are built by people who understand the problem deeply and are obsessive enough to solve it.

What Comes Next

Conduit AI is live and growing. Businesses are deploying AI workforces and seeing results on day one. But this is just the beginning. The vision is a world where any person, regardless of their resources or background, can start and scale a business with the operational infrastructure of a Fortune 500 company.

That world is closer than most people think. And we are building it in public, one deploy at a time.
